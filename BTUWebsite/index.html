HOME

The ATLAS BTU Lab is an inclusive community makerspace facilitating projects at the intersection of Art, Design, Engineering and Architecture.

It serves multiple functions as a general workspace, and workshop host.

WHO?

Anyone* can gain access to BTU. Our goal is to provide the most diverse, inclusive environment for makers of all types in a highly experimental and productive space.

*Due to increased campus security, we are currently unable to sponsor BuffOne Guest cards.

HOW?

New members attend a 20 minute general orientation covering community expectations, safety and equipment.

WHEN?

The orientation* schedule, and all scheduled events can be found on the BTU Calendar.

[*Some things involve separate orientations...like lasers and stuff. Check the calendar and the pages above for your interests.]

Uh..WHAT?

Questions: email BTU Lab at atlasbtulab@gmail.com.

BTU RESOURCES

LASER CUTTING

Laser cutter access requires a one-time orientation. The schedule for this, and all BTU events can be found on the CALENDAR page of the BTU website.

Additional support for students is explained on the LASERS page of the BTU website.

Instructional requirements for faculty needing laser cutters can be found HERE.

3D PRINTING

3D-Printing access requires a one-time, 10-15 minute orientation for all users. This orientation can be scheduled for individuals or classes, upon request.

To learn about available resources or to request an orientation, please visit the 3D PRINTING page of the BTU website.

Instructional requirements for faculty needing 3D printers can be found HERE.

WOOD SHOP

Wood shop access requires a one-time orientation regardless of past experience. The schedule for this and all BTU events can be found on the CALENDAR page of the BTU website.

Additional information about wood shop tools and policies can be found on the WOOD SHOP page of the BTU website.

Instructional requirements for faculty needing the wood shop can be found HERE.

OTHER RESOURCES

BTU has many additional resources, which are explained on the BTU WEBSITE, as well as requirements for any training you might need.

Support is also provided by BTU's amazing student staff, the BTU MISCHIEF MAKERS.

Each of these offer instructional support in many topics, listed on the Mischief Makers page, along with their contact information.

Materials: BTU LAB IS NOT A FREE SUPPLY STORE.

But there are many ways to barter, including displaying your work in BTU, tagging us in social media, offering workshops, volunteering to organize supplies, and more. If you need something, we ask that you give something in return.
